Teaching and Supervision

Artificial Intelligence for Automation

Politecnico di Bari · 2024–2026 · 30 hours in 2024; 60 hours per offering in 2025 and 2026

Course responsibility includes Petri nets, automata, Markov chains, and reinforcement learning, from theoretical foundations to applications. Student projects apply reinforcement learning to automation problems such as planar-arm collision avoidance and scheduling in timed Petri nets.

Control and Security of Cyber-Physical Systems

Politecnico di Bari · 2025 and 2026 · 10 hours per offering

The course examines automation and cyber-physical systems from control and security perspectives, covering formal modeling, Petri nets, automata, and connections between theoretical foundations and real-world applications.
