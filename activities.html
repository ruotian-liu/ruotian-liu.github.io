Activities

EU-Funded Research Projects

CHORUS

Horizon Europe · Work Package Manager and Leader

Coordination of Heterogeneous Actors in Mixed Traffic within CCAM develops a unified orchestration framework for connected, cooperative, and automated mobility. It integrates automated and conventional vehicles with public and shared transport, using secure, AI-enabled traffic management across seven European demonstration sites.

IN2CCAM

Horizon Europe · Project Participant

Enhancing Integration and Interoperability of the CCAM Ecosystem advances connected, cooperative, and automated mobility services through closer integration with traffic management systems. The project operates six living labs across Europe, including Bari.

eCharge4Drivers

Horizon 2020 · Project Participant

eCharge4Drivers improves the electric-vehicle charging experience in cities and on interurban corridors. Its work includes convenient charging options, charging-location planning, mobile charging, lamp-post charging, battery swapping, and guidance for infrastructure investment and regulation.
